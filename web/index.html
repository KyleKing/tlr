How to read this board

Placement: an issue in an active cycle shows in that cycle's column, otherwise its milestone, otherwise Backlog. Capacity heat is a forecast against each person's effective capacity: a base velocity (20 points per cycle by default) cut down by on-call weeks and days out of office, with milestone columns scaled by the weeks in their window. A cell shows 📟 on-call, 🧳 time off, and an "over" badge when committed points exceed that capacity. Hover any ticket for its full title, assignee, milestone, relations, and the reason for a flag.

  • ⚠ slop scans the description for AI tells: dashes, semicolons, checklists, length, and stock phrases. A false positive can be marked "not slop" from the hover card; it stays cleared until the ticket text changes.
  • ⛔ ordering risk means a blocker finishes after the issue it blocks.
  • ◑ missing marks an absent estimate, assignee, or milestone, but only for issues already committed to a cycle.

Filters: double-click a Status or Bucket chip to show only that one (double-click again to restore). "Rows: people / buckets" flips the grid so buckets run down the side when heavy filtering leaves few columns. The board re-fetches every five minutes and on Refresh without touching your filters or scroll position.
